This is a short post reflecting on the ACM's proposal to make the digital library systematically available for training LLMs on the basis that AI is both 1) "da future"; and 2) a delicate little life-form that needs our support; and possibly 3) "or else we'll be left behind".

I used to believe in some nebulous sense that I was depositing things into institutions: my labor, prestigious papers, time and energy in general... all sorts of stuff went into conferences like CHI (where I mostly publish(ed)) and FAccT with hope that it was all going to some collective project and that we could like... cash out from eventually, and do something principled and courageous and ambitious.

Over the last few years I have obviously become disillusioned with that belief; I guess, to continue the metaphor, I'm beginning to wonder if the bank into which I've been depositing is functionally insolvent.

The ACM's folding about LLMs and specifically "AI" is just another (honestly pretty unremarkable) entry in the ledger that makes me wonder "is there any point in continuing to deposit into this account, or should we close it out"

I truly gave up years of my life to help build up this project. I'm not saying I started the ACM I'm not stupid, but anyone who ever reviewed or chaired or even submitted a paper has helped build up this institution so that it could ... do....  what?

if the answer to that "what" question in the face of genocide is "fuckall" and the answer to that "what" question in the face of AI is "fold entirely" then like... what was the fucking point?

either this collective pool we've been depositing into is fundamentally not going to a project we all actually endorse (pro genocide?), or the project has transformed into something that just wants to accumulate deposits for some imaginary future project, but never actually spend it to help us in the here and now

either way the bank is functionally insolvent

if someone tells you they have billions of dollars but they can never make rent because they're saving all that money for when they're living on mars, they're fucking broke in a spiritual way or something if not literally lol
